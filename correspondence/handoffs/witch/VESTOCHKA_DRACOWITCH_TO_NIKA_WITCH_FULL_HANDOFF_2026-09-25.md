# 4. AUTHORIAL MEANING — DO NOT LITERALIZE THE WRONG THING

Useful source:
`04_TRANSLATION_CONTEXT.md`
Library file ID:

Even though it was built for translations, it contains author-level semantic context that is directly relevant to visual storytelling.

## «Ей когти точил»

Not literal manicure / blacksmith gag.

Meaning:
- he taught her to defend herself;
- to “show claws”;
- push back;
- protect herself;
- not let people mistreat her.

For visual work the word “claws” can remain as a fantasy/object metaphor, but the emotional meaning is **capacity / agency / defense learned or enabled**.

## «Она колдовала — всё больше себя»

Not literal spellcasting.

Meaning:
- increasing focus on herself;
- her own wishes/interests;
- self-directed attention;
- selfish/self-absorbed concentration.

This is exactly why current Panel 2 avoids magic FX.

## «Посылала дракона под хвост»

Humorous dragon-world euphemism for a rough dismissal / “go to hell / fuck off”.

Do not build a literal tail visual gag.

## «Он расстался, сошёлся — и снова ушёл»

Same woman:
`separated -> reconciled -> separated again`.

Do not visualize as multiple partners.

## «Корысть примеряла, как новое манто»

Multiple simultaneous meanings:
- benefit / consumer attitude;
- trying on a mercantile role;
- appropriating what Dragon gave as if it were simply her own luxury.

This is one reason the current Panel 4 uses a mantle-like textile but does not reduce the idea to “woman wearing coat”.

## «Он драконил»

Layered author verb:
- raged;
- behaved like a dragon;
- snarled;
- provoked;
- tortured himself;
- acted out.

Do not flatten it to one literal motion if it ever becomes visual.

## «Она знала расклад»

Witch/tarot semantic chain:
`witch -> divination -> tarot spread -> analysis -> calculation -> deliberate awareness`.

Meaning:
- she understood what she was doing;
- could predict consequences;
- used the situation consciously.

## «Он всегда был подхватом. Она — никогда»

Key relationship fact:
- he catches;
- covers gaps;
- carries part of load;
- solves;
- supports;
- she does not reciprocate that support.

This is the entire core of current Panel 4.

## «Я устал быть крылом»

He is tired of being:
- support;
- safety net;
- inspiration;
- motivator;
- direction;
- shoulder;
- literal/metaphorical wing;
- donor of part of his own “dragon” capacity.

## «Имя он выжег»

Intentional attempt to remove her from memory/life, not a decorative burned-paper trick.

## «А сердце не взялось огнём»

The deliberate erasure does not delete attachment/emotion.

## «Ведьмы брали как должное. Чуйка: “Один”»

After her there were other women / “witches” who also took care/support as expected.

“Чуйка” means intuition / subconscious pattern-recognition / almost oracular knowledge, not merely “I feel lonely right now”.

## «Крылья? Когти? Манто? Всегда это было моё.»

Her final position:
- she claims as inherently hers what Dragon sees as partly enabled/provided by him;
- she does not accept his authorship of her stronger self.

This is why the ending belongs to **her ownership of identity**, not to Dragon’s accusation.

## Refrain dramatic arc

Author-context source defines the progression:
1. sincere surprise;
2. doubt/testing;
3. recognition;
4. trying the identity on;
5. acceptance;
6. assertion;
7. full final appropriation: “I always was this.”

The visual heel/step system is the body-language equivalent of this arc.
