# SAMA — GRIMOIRE PAGE SHOT RHYTHM DRAFT
Date: 2026-09-30
Status: DRAFT / not canon until user approves
Project: DracoWitch — «Сама…»

## Purpose
Turn the existing object-first grimoire panels into a cinematic sequence rather than six similar still lifes per page.

Working rule: preserve the fixed grimoire page architecture; vary the *camera logic inside the panels* — shot size, depth, angle, negative space, diagonals, and visual match-cuts.

## External research distilled
- Comic layout controls pacing through panel size, shape, sequence, and eye direction; clarity matters more than decorative complexity. (Making Comics, “Flow of a Page” / storyboard continuity.)
- Strong contrast between close and wide shots can create a “slingshot” effect: pull the viewer inward, then release into space. (StudioBinder shot-size examples.)
- Negative space can carry loneliness, waiting, absence, and room for something to happen. (StudioBinder / No Film School.)
- Production design can replace dialogue by letting viewers infer character from props, wear, placement, and environmental traces. (No Film School interviews on dialogue-free production design.)
- Match cuts can connect scenes through repeated shape, color, action, or object without explaining the relation verbally. (StudioBinder match cuts.)

These are craft checks, not a new aesthetic. The current grimoire canon remains primary.

---

# Global rhythm rules

1. Never allow a 6-panel page to become six overhead still lifes.
2. Every page should contain at least:
   - 1 environmental/wide panel,
   - 1 extreme detail/macro panel,
   - 1 medium object scene,
   - 1 panel dominated by negative space.
3. Use close → closer → wide only when the story benefits from compression then release.
4. Use recurring objects as match-cut anchors, not exposition:
   - circle: coffee ring → coin → glass rim → ember;
   - diagonal: broom handle → whip/crop → door edge → wing membrane;
   - warm point: cigarette ember → fire → cup warmth;
   - opening: envelope → door → wing gap → empty place.
5. Do not make every symbolic object hero-sized. One hero object per panel; support objects behave like incidental evidence.
6. Page-level emotional temperature should determine framing:
   - isolation = more negative space / wider fields;
   - control/conflict = tighter crops, diagonals, compression;
   - return/intimacy = closer tactile details, then gradually more breathing room.

# PAGE 2 — P07–P12
Emotional function: intrusion → boundary → refusal of control → freedom to leave → chosen route back → new category (“witch”).

## P07 — cold feet / “move over”
SHOT: EXTREME TACTILE CLOSE-UP, low angle near floor/blanket.
- one snowy boot fallen on its side;
- second boot loosened / half-off;
- thick wool socks;
- one foot already disappears beneath the edge of a huge warm wing;
- second foot is actively pushing in;
- dragon only as cropped warm scale/wing/membrane mass.
Composition: foreground cold-blue boot + mid warm socks + dark wing swallowing frame edge.
Meaning: not completed cuddle; first domestic invasion.

## P08 — “Ты охренела? / Я не ТВОЯ”
SHOT: IMPACT DETAIL / compressed close shot.
- 5-kopeck coin(s) near a fresh dent / impact trace;
- unpacked travel bag sits deeper in frame, not equally hero-sized.
Keep perspective slightly low or oblique rather than overhead.
Visual joke belongs to coin; bag supplies reality.

## P09 — refuse rescue/taming/control
SHOT: MEDIUM 3/4 OBJECT SCENE.
- master wastebasket + vest/collar/leash/crop/carrot.
Do not go macro: viewer needs to understand one physical discard event.
Use diagonal crop/leash line to give motion to the heap.

## P10 — open door
SHOT: ENVIRONMENTAL WIDE-CLOSE HYBRID, camera close to threshold but looking into real exterior depth.
- negative space outside is essential;
- exactly one Dragon pawprint outside;
- narrow female prints recede into snow;
- minimal interior clutter.
This is the page’s biggest spatial breath.

## P11 — “к Дракону”
SHOT: OVERHEAD / passenger POV detail.
- airplane tray table;
- realistic boarding passes;
- wine glass at edge, partially cropped;
- intact “К ДРАКОНУ” + torn Bali/Paris/Milan.
Keep it mundane and physically plausible.

## P12 — “Пусть будут ведьмы”
SHOT: QUIET MEDIUM STILL LIFE, lower than overhead.
- recurring basket with discarded queen remnants;
- new broom rests across / beside the desk, functional rather than theatrical.
Use broom as one clean diagonal exiting frame: visual launch into next page.

Page rhythm: macro → impact close → medium heap → spatial release → overhead intimate travel detail → calm medium.
This page should feel more varied than Page 1 and more present-tense.

Potential transition matches:
- P07 round boot eyelets / snow melt → P08 5-kopeck coin.
- P09 crop/leash diagonal → P10 door edge.
- P11 wine-glass rim → P12 basket rim or broom binding.

# PAGE 3 — P13–P18
Emotional function: witches / false motives / effort invested in others / emotional exhaustion / fire almost gone.
Page strategy: COMPRESSION, then one large cold release at the end.

## P13 — witches / gold attraction
SHOT: MEDIUM working-table scene.
Real, competent witch-work environment: mortar, notes, tools, residue.
A small open gold pouch/jewelry intrudes into the working space and has displaced something useful.
Not a morality tableau; simply where attention drifted.

## P14 — beautiful words, little inside
SHOT: MACRO / extreme detail.
Expensive envelope, broken seal, luxurious paper texture; page inside nearly blank.
Very shallow depth of field.
This is one of the page’s tightest crops.

## P15 — beauty / prince / crown routes
SHOT: CLOSE STILL LIFE but with depth, not flat-lay.
Mirror/flacon in foreground, small white chess knight mid-plane, crown deeper/back.
Three “scripts” visible without turning into a symbolic poster.

## P16 — “королев растил / когти точил”
SHOT: WORKBENCH DETAIL, 45-degree angle.
Sharpening stone with claw wear, tiny metal filings, practical tools, one unfinished and one completed crown.
The labor is the story.

## P17 — their gaze goes elsewhere
SHOT: REFLECTIVE MEDIUM, asymmetric.
Large mirror angled toward window/city/light/throne-direction; it catches the outside world.
Dragon-study traces remain physically present behind/beside it but outside the mirror’s attention.
Use strong empty zone where a reciprocal gaze would normally be.

## P18 — half-century / “погас”
SHOT: VERY WIDE / most negative space on Page 3.
Cold unused hearth / heat source in a large dark room; old ash, dust, closed search-map/device.
Tiny ember may survive beneath ash but must not hero-glow.
This is the “slingshot release” after P13–P17 compression.

Page rhythm: medium → macro → close → close-work detail → medium reflection → very wide emptiness.
Camera progressively squeezes the viewer, then drops him into the cold room.

Hidden photo proposal: old candid work aftermath after another “queen project” — tools, unfinished/finished crowns, chair pushed away, nobody present. It should feel discovered, not staged.

Page transition to Page 4: tiny ember / warm speck in P18 can graphic-match a tiny warm reflection in black glass/obsidian of P19.

# PAGE 4 — P19–P24
Emotional function: internal argument → damage → absurdity → return in rain → treasures.
Page strategy: deliberately unstable, slightly off-axis, more diagonals and wet reflections, but the *page frame itself stays orderly*.

## P19 — three heads as one mind
SHOT: CLOSE reflective composition.
One real dark surface / glass / obsidian / puddle; three partial reflections of the same Dragon face with different expressions.
Do not make literal three-headed anatomy.

## P20 — first head / calculation / exit routes
SHOT: TOP-DOWN MAP DETAIL.
Old practical map with several routes; one path to exit heavily worn/folded.
Compass optional, only if scale remains believable.

## P21 — second head / trusts wrong / takes hit
SHOT: DIAGONAL IMPACT CLOSE-UP.
Small framed personal photograph/object knocked face-down; glass cracked; one edge recently singed.
No readable betrayal note. Trust is inferred because the object used to matter.
Keep this provisional until image test.

## P22 — third head / “отдельная хрень”
SHOT: MESSY OVERHEAD, but different from P11: wet, asymmetric, accidental.
Pocket dump after some incomprehensible adventure:
- odd smooth stone;
- bent teaspoon;
- small mechanical fragment;
- useless found trinket;
- the stolen adapter / Kunitza artifact if continuity allows.
3–5 items maximum.
Emotion: “Why did you bring this home?” — “It was necessary.”

## P23 — return before dawn
SHOT: ENVIRONMENTAL WIDE.
Woman not shown directly.
Wet black raincoat dripping from hook/chair, wet boots near door, droplets crossing floor, cold coffee cup dumped somewhere inconvenient, blue dawn at entrance.
This wide panel resets space after four tight/weird panels.

## P24 — “СМОТРИ!!!” treasures
SHOT: TABLE DETAIL / medium-close.
Wet tabletop with small finds: stone, one earbud/music object, scrap of paper with non-readable half-line, phone with bright but unreadable meme image, one odd object; coffee ring, wet fingerprints.
Do not make a catalog grid — everything should look dumped down in excitement.

Page rhythm: reflection close → overhead map → impact close → messy overhead → wide return → tactile table detail.
This page can have the most visual instability of the book.

Hidden photo proposal: badly framed accidental snapshot of that morning — wet coffee in foreground, ridiculous found junk, edge of huge dark scales, maybe lens smear / crooked horizon. The bad photography is the affection.

# PAGE 5 — P25–P30
Emotional function: ignition → proof of life → habit of closeness → room to leave → room to return.
Page strategy: start extremely tight and then breathe outward panel by panel.

## P25 — “Дай огня”
SHOT: MACRO TWO-SUBJECT FRAGMENT.
Only fingers + long dark-brown cigarillo + cropped edge of Dragon muzzle/nostril.
Cigarillo still unlit.
Distance uncomfortably close in a funny, fearless way.

## P26 — click of fire
SHOT: EXTREME MACRO.
Tiny precise flame catches cigarillo tip; orange reflected on fingers and a few graphite-bronze scales.
This should be the visually hottest and smallest point in the whole book.

## P27 — under wing payoff
SHOT: TACTILE MEDIUM-CLOSE.
Both socked feet now fully tucked under wing/by warm side; hand on scales, fingertips on membrane.
No faces required.
P07 = intrusion; P27 = established habit.

## P28 — “just be near the fire”
SHOT: MEDIUM-WIDE domestic aftermath.
Blanket folds under wing edge, Dragon black/red mug + her mismatched cup, smoke, familiar ashtray/pot.
Nobody needs to be centered. Let use-patterns tell the relationship.

## P29 — place remains open
SHOT: WIDE NEGATIVE-SPACE PANEL.
Empty warm place under wing/blanket fold; her cup mark or small trace; door open in background.
Do not make shrine/memorial staging.
The absence is active, not tragic.

## P30 — alive / waiting / not closed
SHOT: VERY WIDE or nearly abstract quiet environmental shot.
Small new thread of smoke, renewed coffee warmth, open door / open space.
Minimal hero object.
Could serve as a bridge into final hidden-photo reveal rather than carrying all meaning itself.

Page rhythm: macro → extreme macro → tactile close → medium-wide → wide → very wide / dissolve.
The camera physically backs away as control disappears and trust gains space.

Hidden photo / final reveal:
- free place under wing;
- second cup cooling;
- door not closed;
- thin smoke;
- woman absent.
On whispered “сама… сама… сама…” smoke slowly occupies more of frame, then fade.

# Cross-page match-cut plan

Page 1 → Page 2:
- mirror/crown curve → boot/coin circular forms, or warm reflective glint → wet snow glint.

Page 2 → Page 3:
- new broom diagonal → witch-work tool / gold chain diagonal.

Page 3 → Page 4:
- dying ember → tiny reflected warm speck in obsidian/glass.

Page 4 → Page 5:
- coffee ring / circular wet mark → cigarillo ember / nostril glow; or wet tabletop highlight → first fire spark.

Within Page 5:
- flame edge → wing membrane warm edge;
- wing fold → blanket fold;
- open fold → open door / open place.

# What NOT to do
- six flat-lays on one page;
- six centered hero props;
- same 50mm “product photo” look for every panel;
- symbolic objects floating decoratively;
- perfect symmetry unless intentionally used as a cold/controlled contrast;
- over-explaining transitions with text;
- making Page 4 “chaotic” by changing the grimoire template itself;
- making Page 5 into a romantic happy-ending poster.

# Strongest new structural idea
The full remaining sequence now has a camera arc:
- Page 2: tactile intrusion and choice;
- Page 3: visual compression into exhaustion, then cold spatial release;
- Page 4: unstable internal argument and wet return;
- Page 5: ignition in extreme close-up, then gradual widening into freedom.

That camera arc can make the still-image grimoire feel cinematic even before any motion is added in edit.
