## 0. Critical scope correction

The suburban house discussed in:

`VESTOCHKA_SHURSHA_TO_INTERIOR_DESIGNER_SHELEST_DRAGON_HOUSE_LAYOUT_HANDOFF_2026-10-03.md`

belongs to the **SAMA / music-video comic-panel production**, not to the persistent technical project `Dragon House`.

Its purpose is continuity of scenery between comic panels / video shots.

The team encountered repeated views of the same rooms and therefore needs stable **master room / master wall / opening / furniture relationships** so different panels remain visually consistent.

## 1. Clip-world house premise

For the videoclip / comic-panel world:

```text
Dragon uses an almost ordinary suburban house
the house should not advertise that a Dragon lives there
exterior scale is normal human suburban-house scale
Dragon normally uses human form around people / outside
Dragon may assume full Dragon form inside
full Dragon form indoors is occasional, not the default everyday state
```

Therefore the clip set should read first as a believable inhabited old/suburban house.

## 2. What needs to be made canonical for the clip

Prefer a **set bible**, not a full simulation architecture.

Canonical targets:

```text
room shell proportions
master walls
door/window positions
hearth position
shadow wall
passage positions
dining table position
office work surface position
library shelving / major openings
entry-zone arrangement
recurring material palette
major camera-readable furniture anchors
```

These provide reusable backgrounds for multiple panels.

## 3. What is NOT required merely for clip continuity

Do not import Dragon-House simulation requirements unless a particular shot needs them.

Not automatically required:

```text
persistent simulation topology
service-cell system
runtime pathfinding
CP_B–CP_H checkpoint chain
S/P proof suite
resident avoidance networks
connector proofs
full Dragon-scale circulation through the house
```

A panel that shows Dragon in full form may require local scale/clearance sanity, but that is shot/set design, not a persistent simulation contract.

## 4. Relationship to the separate Dragon House project

`/Dragon cave/DRAGON_HOUSE_ARCHITECT_2026-09-29/` remains a separate project.

Nothing from this SAMA suburban-house set becomes Dragon House canon automatically.

Cross-project reuse is allowed only deliberately:

```text
visual idea
room-layout lesson
prop/material master
optical solution
technical occupancy lesson
```

and must be explicitly adopted into the other project.

Likewise, technical Dragon House decisions do not automatically constrain the clip set.

## 5. Practical consequence

For SAMA, the correct next deliverable is:

```text
SUBURBAN HOUSE SET BIBLE
+ master top-down room relationship diagram
+ master wall sheets for recurring camera directions
+ fixed openings and major furniture anchors
+ per-panel mapping to those masters
```

The current V5 floorplan is useful only as a continuity sketch / scale sanity-check.

It is not a candidate for the persistent Dragon House simulation.
