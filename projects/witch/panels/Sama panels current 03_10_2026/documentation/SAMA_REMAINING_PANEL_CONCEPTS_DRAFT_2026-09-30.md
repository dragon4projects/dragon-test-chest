**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Status:** DRAFT / idea pool, not canon until Dragon approves individual panels or page structure.  
**Project:** DracoWitch — «Сама…» / grimoire-panels branch.  
**Primary lyric source:** Google Drive native Doc **«Сама»**, file ID `1zbVv1ZO1vJ3P0OQPQiFRhTzhp2csVqtkKFHdlxJg7HY`. Use the first/current complete lyric block in that document; later appended older variants are historical and must not silently replace it.

## 0. What stays locked

- Master page is already locked; do not regenerate it.
- 6 panel slots per page.
- Panels are separate photorealistic assets composited into the master page.
- Current language: a witch's private grimoire / diary made of pasted photorealistic memories.
- Default rule: no full Dragon or Witch characters in the panels. Tell the story through objects, traces, aftermath, partial contact, doors, tickets, ash, snow, cups, papers, clothing and changes of state.
- Physical reality matters. No magically shrinking objects, floating symbolism, showroom-polish or fantasy scrapbook clutter.
- Prefer one or two sharp material ideas over a collage of metaphors.
- Recurring props should keep identity and gradually reveal the Dragon House.
- The older full-character keyframe plan remains a useful source of dramatic beats, but does not override the current object-first grimoire language.

# 2. P07 — COLD FEET / «Согрей меня» — revised concept

**Current status:** still IDEA_MASTER. Do not lock yet.

The key structural insight: **P07 should not spend the late-song payoff too early.** The first chorus is the request / cheeky attempt to get warm. The late bridge is where she is actually settled under the wing.

## Preferred concept: “toe theft” / cheeky entry
Camera low, close to floor / stone / rug. No faces, no couple portrait.

- One snow-covered winter boot already kicked off and lying on its side.
- Second boot still upright but unlaced / half-off.
- Small wet snow clumps and melt marks lead inward.
- Thick, rough wool socks; one toe/heel slightly damp and darker.
- Warm blanket rumpled over calves, not beautifully arranged.
- One socked foot has already disappeared under the edge of a huge dark wing / against a warm scaled flank.
- The second socked foot is **still pushing under** — crooked, practical, a little insolent.
- Dragon is only a massive warm dark fragment: wing edge, membrane, scale texture, flank. No head, no full silhouette.
- Cold blue-white near boots; restrained amber / warm brown only under the wing.

**Emotional read:** “подвинься, я уже лезу,” not “romantic fantasy cuddle.”  
**Tone:** cozy impudence; physical domesticity; trust without ceremony.

### Variants to test later
A. **Before contact** — both socked feet under blanket, wing edge close but not yet touched. Most restrained; preserves maximum payoff for P27.  
B. **Toe theft** — one foot under, second in motion. **Preferred balance.**  
C. **Fully tucked** — save for P27 / late bridge, not first chorus.

### Avoid
- steam coming from feet;
- cute heart-shaped framing;
- symmetrical “couple” pose;
- full Dragon and woman;
- excessive fireplace glow / Hallmark warmth;
- delicate fashion socks: they should look actually warm.

# 3. P08 — «Ты охренела? — Да. Я не ТВОЯ» — refinement note

Keep the current three-part logic but make it materially cleaner:

- Soviet 5-kopeck coin(s) remain the private “глаза по пять копеек” joke.
- The punch / “ДА” should be a believable **fist dent in a thin metal travel locker, trunk, cabinet panel or luggage shell**, not a floating symbolic impact graphic.

- If any “не твоя” text survives, keep it as a small private tag / scribble, not a poster slogan.

Read: **I came. I am here. That still does not make me property.**

# 5. PAGE 3 — P13–P18

Theme: **good taste is not the same as good motive; Dragon stops searching and almost stops burning.**

## P13 — witches can want gold too
Lyric beat: “Пусть будут ведьмы… Но ведьмы тоже на золото падки…”

A competent, lived-in witch workbench. Real working things: stained glassware, mortar, notebook, metal tool, dried herb residue. But one small open pouch/case of gold or jewelry has displaced the actual work at the center.

Important: skill is real. Do not caricature the witch as stupid or greedy. The tension is simply that even an impressive person can be oriented toward the wrong thing.

**One-frame question:** what is the thing she actually reaches for?

## P14 — beautiful words / emptiness inside
Lyric beat: “цена красивых слов… прячут пустое глубоко внутри.”

A beautiful heavy envelope / card with expensive paper, wax seal, careful exterior. It has been opened. Inside: almost nothing — a nearly blank sheet, maybe a few faint meaningless marks, no readable prose.

Alternative if blank paper feels too on-the-nose: a beautiful lacquered little box, elaborately wrapped, opened to reveal only packing tissue and an empty fitted recess.

**Preferred:** the letter, because letters already belong to the project’s artifact language.

## P15 — beauty / prince / crown
Lyric beat: one wants eternal beauty; another a prince on a horse; third wants a crown, not him.

Do **not** show three women. Make an abandoned dressing shelf / low bench with exactly three relics:
- small hand mirror + one anti-age cosmetic vial / comb (beauty);
- a single white chess knight (prince on horse, dry private joke rather than literal horse);
- crown.

Keep it dusty / used / left behind, not arranged as a symbolic museum tableau.

## P16 — «Он королев растил… точил когти»

Dragon’s workbench after the work is done:
- a heavy claw-sharpening stone / file with deep grooves;
- jeweler/metalworking tongs or small hammer;
- one crown in progress and one finished crown set aside;
- tiny filings / black grit.

No hands. No Dragon. The labor is the character.

Read: he invested craft, time and power into making others stronger.

## P17 — their gaze goes anywhere but Dragon

**Preferred object translation:** an ornate standing mirror angled toward a bright city / throne-room / crowd beyond the window. Behind/near the mirror, the Dragon’s huge black-red mug and one familiar warm place remain ignored and partly out of focus.

The mirror catches the outside ambition; it does **not** catch the Dragon’s place.

Keep it quiet. No text “not him.”

Alternative: empty chair facing the window, while the Dragon’s study is behind it. Simpler but weaker.

## P18 — search stopped / flame nearly gone

Pair two states in one physical location without clutter:
- a cold, blackened hearth/firebox or Dragon-scale brazier that clearly has not burned for years;
- nearby a closed, dust-filmed map / compass / face-down phone, no active “search.”

The key is **ash and disuse**, not sadness props.

### Hidden-photo reveal for Page 3 — suggestion
When the last panel leaves, reveal a close dark photograph of the dead hearth. Deep under grey ash, **one tiny ember is still red**. This quietly carries “Но где-то глубоко всё ещё было горячо” without another panel screaming the metaphor.

# 6. PAGE 4 — P19–P24

Theme: **his internal argument, then her return with rain and useless treasures.**

## P19 — three heads, without a literal three-headed monster
Lyric beat: “У него три головы”.

A black obsidian / smoked-glass surface, dark window or puddle catches **three partial reflections of the same Dragon face**, while the real Dragon remains mostly out of frame.

- one reflection cold / analytical;
- one with faint ember warmth / impulsive;
- one slightly crooked / amused / dangerous-to-common-sense.

This is one of the few places where Dragon-face fragments are justified. Still no permanent three-neck anatomy.

## P20 — first head: mind / sees lie / knows the exit

Old map on the Dragon-study desk, not fantasy treasure-map cliché: practical folded map with worn edges. Several routes exist, but one exit path is heavily rubbed / repeatedly traced. A single claw scratch or blunt graphite mark stops at the door/edge.

Optional: one brass compass. Nothing more.

Read: he always knows how to leave.

## P21 — second head: fire / believes wrong / takes the hit

Aftermath, not the action:
- scorched edge of a beautiful wax-sealed note;
- a fresh dent in a nearby thin metal surface / cracked signet / snapped clasp;
- ember still faintly hot.

It should feel like “believed, lunged, got hit, still thinks it mattered,” not generic heartbreak.

This panel is less locked than P19/P20; test visually before canon.

## P22 — third head: “separate bullshit” / damage but maybe worth it

An emptied wet pocket / bag on a table after some inexplicable outing. A few specific absurd trophies, not fantasy loot:
- odd smooth stone;
- bent teaspoon / tiny stolen café object;
- obsolete adapter / strange little connector;
- black ribbon / small found mechanical piece;
- one ticket stub with no readable text.

A little mud / rain. One item may obviously be useless but cherished.

The humor is: the first head will itemize losses; the second is already saying “worth it.”

Do not overfill. 3–5 things max.

## P23 — pre-dawn door / she came back wet

Object-only translation of the old master story beat:
- black raincoat dripping over a chair / hook;
- soaked boots by the threshold;
- small puddle / drip trail;
- paper cup of cold coffee, half-full, lid slightly warped;
- door recently used / dawn-blue light leaking in.

No person needed. Her energy should be visible in how carelessly things landed.

## P24 — «СМОТРИ!!! Что я ещё нашла!» / treasures

A table/detail shot after she has dumped her “treasures” out:
- small stone;
- one earbud / tiny player / waveform-like device without text;
- scrap of paper with only an unreadable half-line / marks;
- phone face-up with a bright meme-like image but **no legible text**;
- one tiny found shard/object catching light;
- wet fingerprints / coffee ring.

This should feel like an excited pocket-dump, not an evidence table.

### Hidden-photo reveal for Page 4 — suggestion
Behind the moving panels: a candid-feeling dawn image of the threshold **after** arrival — wet floor, raincoat, cold coffee, warm interior waking up. If later we permit a human fragment, a blurred shoulder/hair edge can exist, but object-only works.

# 7. PAGE 5 — P25–P30

Theme: **fire returns, then warmth becomes ordinary rather than promised.**

## P25 — «Дай огня»

Partial-body exception, because the gesture is the joke.

Close detail:
- woman’s fingers holding a long dark-brown cigarillo / cigarette;
- filter toward her, working end toward the Dragon;
- the Dragon appears only as a dark graphite-bronze snout / nostril edge entering frame;
- cigarillo is **unlit**.

The physical distance should be absurdly casual and close. No glamour manicure shot.

## P26 — “click of fire” / proof he is not extinguished

Same visual family, tighter:
- tiny precise flame from off-frame Dragon breath / nostril;
- just the cigarillo tip catches;
- warm reflection on fingers and a few graphite-bronze scales;
- not a fireball.

This panel can later be punched brighter / faster for “ЖГИИИИИИ!!!” rather than generating a separate giant-fire image.

## P27 — cold feet payoff / now fully under the wing

This is the **earned full version** that P07 must not steal.

- thick wool-socked feet fully tucked beneath a heavy wing edge / against warm scale side;
- one hand rests naturally on scales;
- fingertips of the other touch the thin wing membrane;
- blanket crushed around them;
- Dragon only as massive tactile fragments, not a full creature;
- no faces necessary.

Warm, ordinary, slightly indecent in its familiarity — not erotic, not saccharine.

Visual rhyme: P07 = “I’m getting in.” P27 = “I’m already here.”

## P28 — «Не надо обещаний… просто будь рядом с огнём»

Quiet aftermath / domestic still life:
- rumpled blanket disappearing under wing edge;
- Dragon’s black mug with red interior;
- her second cup nearby, not matching;
- one lit cigarillo / cigarette resting in the recurring cauldron ashtray;
- faint smoke;
- no rings, no vows, no paired-heart objects.

The intimacy is that nobody tidied up.

## P29 — “maybe she disappears / no one takes that place”

Avoid repeating P10’s departing footprints.

Show **the reserved absence itself**:
- under-wing shelter / familiar warm corner is empty;
- blanket edge still folded or naturally shaped as if a place exists there;
- one old cup ring / small personal trace remains;
- door in deep background is ajar, but not the hero.

The frame should not read “mourning shrine.” It is simply a place not reassigned.

## P30 — final «САМА» / waiting without calling

Best as the **last moving panel that exposes the final hidden photograph** rather than as a crowded climax.

Panel P30 itself can be a very small, almost comic beat:
- fresh thin smoke rising again from the Dragon side / ashtray;
- coffee renewed;
- maybe the tiny fire reflection returning to a familiar metal object.

Then P30 leaves its slot and reveals the actual final photograph below.

### Final hidden photo — preferred ending
Quiet Dragon House interior / tower room:
- the place under the wing is **free**;
- a second cup is cooling nearby;
- door is not closed;
- faint Dragon smoke drifts through the frame;
- no cage, no chain, no “come back” note;
- no woman present.

On the final whispered “сама… сама… сама…” the smoke gradually fills the frame and fades to black.

Meaning: not “she stayed forever.” **She can return because the place exists without becoming a demand.**

# 8. Refrain / repetition strategy

The song repeats the chorus, but the visuals do not need to pretend the words are new.

## Chorus 1
P07–P11 as proposition / imagined rules / first statement of the relationship grammar.

## Chorus 2
Revisit the same images with:
- warmer grade;
- slightly closer crops;
- slower, more settled motion;
- no new symbolic objects.

The viewer recognizes the same rules, but now they have history behind them.

## Final chorus
Use short visual echoes rather than replaying every panel in full:
- P07/P27 rhyme (feet entering → feet settled);
- P09 object-control rejection flash;
- P10 open door flash;
- P11 chosen route flash;
- P26 tiny flame returning;
- then final hidden photo / free place.

This makes the repeated chorus behave like memory, not duplication.

# 10. Why this direction fits the established project

The project already works best when an object carries an action forward: the same basket changes contents; the open door carries a decision; the ticket carries a choice; the recurring mug/ashtray makes the Dragon House feel inhabited. Continue that logic rather than inventing new decorative symbolism for every line.

Useful rule for every new panel:

> **Ask what physical evidence would remain five minutes after the lyric happened. Photograph that.**

If the answer needs six symbols to explain itself, simplify it.

# 12. External research note

A quick production-design sanity check supports the direction already discovered in this project: lived-in sets read through a small number of **story anchors, character traces and functional objects**, and recurring used/left-behind objects can carry action across scenes without exposition. This is not a new rule imposed on the project; it simply matches the direction already working here.
